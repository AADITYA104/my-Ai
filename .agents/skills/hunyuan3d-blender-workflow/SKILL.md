### Hunyuan3D-2: Blender Automation & Pipeline Bridge
- Connects local generative 3D workers to Blender 4.x viewport.
- Automates GLB importing, armature rigging, subdivision surface modifiers, and Cycles/Eevee photorealistic rendering.
- Batch generates turntable animations (MP4/GIF) and Three.js optimized assets with Draco compression.
